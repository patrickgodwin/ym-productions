## 🎨 Design Highlights

### Color Scheme
- **Background:** Pure black (#000000) with subtle gradients
- **Accent:** Gold (#C4A853) for branding and CTAs
- **Text:** White with varying opacity for hierarchy
- **Hover States:** Gold highlights for interactive elements

### Typography
- **Hero Headline:** Playfair Display, 2.5-4rem (responsive)
- **Section Headers:** Playfair Display, 2-3.5rem (responsive)
- **Body Text:** Inter, 14-16px
- **Labels:** Inter, 12-14px with letter-spacing for premium feel

### Responsive Breakpoints
- **Desktop:** 1400px+ (full grid layouts)
- **Tablet:** 768px-1024px (single column hero, adjusted grids)
- **Mobile:** <768px (hamburger menu, stacked layouts)
- **Small Mobile:** <480px (compact padding, larger touch targets)
