# 🚀 DEPLOY YOUR MOMENT PRODUCTIONS NOW

## The Fastest Way (5 minutes)

### Step 1: Log into Cloudflare
Go to: **https://dash.cloudflare.com/**
(Create account if needed - it's free)

### Step 2: Create a Pages Project
1. Click **Pages** in the left sidebar
2. Click **Create a project** → **Direct upload**
3. Give it a name: `ym-productions`
4. Click **Next**

### Step 3: Upload the Website
1. You'll see a box that says "Drag and drop your project files"
2. **Download this entire folder** to your computer
3. Drag all the files into the Cloudflare box
   - index.html
   - styles.css
   - script.js
   - _redirects
   - All other files in this folder

### Step 4: Deploy!
1. Click the **Deploy site** button
2. Wait 30 seconds...
3. **Your website is now LIVE!**
